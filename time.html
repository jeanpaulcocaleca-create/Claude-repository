One-time setup needed

The time clock needs its tables and the manager-approval functions in Supabase. It takes about a minute:

  1. Open your Supabase project → SQL Editor → New query.
  2. Apply the pending database migrations, in order, as described in docs/MIGRACIONES.md.
  3. Come back here, reload, and make yourself the first manager under Hours & team.

Time clock

Start here: set up the team

There is no manager yet. Three steps and the clock is ready:

  1. Add the first manager (GM) with a name and a PIN. No approval is needed for this one.
  2. Add each team member with their own PIN, under Hours & team.
  3. To clock in, each person taps their name and types their PIN on the keypad.

Forgot a PIN later? The owner login changes any PIN from Hours & team, no other PIN needed.

Tap your name, type your PIN. Green means you're on the clock.
