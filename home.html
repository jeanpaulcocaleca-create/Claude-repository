Your café, one tap away

POS Ring up sales at the counter — cash, card or SINPE, recorded instantly. Sales What you sold today, this week, this month — best sellers and CSV for the accountant. Insights Trends month by month, busy hours, what each product leaves, suppliers and what to order soon. Menu Manager Change prices, photos and items — updates the website and TV board in a minute. Inventory What is on the shelf, what each sale uses, and what to order before it runs out. Accounting The books: the day's entries, expenses, reports and exports for the accountant.
