The story

A green mountain, a cup of peace

Monteverde was not built for hurry. Every cup we pour carries a century of this mountain: farmers, pacifists, scientists, and a forest that drinks from the clouds.

  • 1900s

    The first farms

    Costa Rican families settle the green mountain, planting coffee and raising dairy on slopes that touch the clouds.

  • 1948

    A nation chooses peace

    Costa Rica abolishes its army. The country puts down its weapons for good.

  • 1951

    The Quakers arrive

    Forty four Quakers leave Alabama, choosing Costa Rica because it has no army. They buy land in the cloud forest and name it Monteverde, the green mountain.

  • 1972

    The forest protected

    The Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve is founded. The community protects the watershed instead of clearing it, and the world comes to walk under its canopy.

  • Today

    Your cup

    Hanging Garden continues the story: slow coffee, hanging plants, and a table waiting for you between the clouds.

Peace. Simplicity. Stewardship. Community.

Good to know

Questions people ask before they visit

Hours, coffee, payment, what to wear: the answers we give at the counter, here before you arrive.

Where is the best coffee in Monteverde?

Ask ten guides in Santa Elena and you get ten answers, so here is ours: the best coffee in Monteverde is grown here, roasted here and brewed slowly. At Hanging Garden Café we pour coffee from El Trapiche, a family farm on this mountain, roasted locally and ground for each cup. Read how we choose it on our coffee page.

What time do you open? I want coffee before the cloud forest reserve.

We open at 6:00 every day, one hour before the Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve opens its gate at 7:00. Coffee, a warm croissant or an empanada, and you are on the trail before the crowds. We close at 19:00.

Where can I have breakfast in Santa Elena before a tour?

Right here, from 6:00. Sandwiches such as the Mano de Piedra with Costa Rican beef, empanadas, croissants, banana bread and a chorreado coffee. Leaving before dawn? Order an Adventure Box by 20:00 the night before and pick it up from 6:30.

Where is Hanging Garden Café, and how far is it from the reserve?

We are in Monteverde, on the road through Santa Elena, Puntarenas, Costa Rica. The Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve is about 10 minutes away by car, and the centre of Santa Elena is a short walk. Tap "Get directions" in the visit section and Google Maps takes you to the door.

Is Monteverde coffee really that good?

Yes, and there is a reason. Monteverde sits at about 1,400 metres, where cool nights make the coffee cherry ripen slowly and build sugar. That is why a Monteverde cup tastes sweet and clean, with chocolate and citrus notes. Our coffee page explains altitude, harvest and roast in plain words.

Is there a coffee tour near Santa Elena, and how long does it take?

Yes. Several family farms around Monteverde run coffee tours of two to three hours, from the plant to the roaster, usually with sugar cane and chocolate too. If you only have twenty minutes, come and taste the same mountain in a cup: we serve El Trapiche coffee and are happy to tell you how it is made.

Can I buy Monteverde coffee beans to take home?

Ask at the counter. We sell what we brew when we have it, whole bean or ground, and we can tell you which farm it comes from and when it was roasted. It packs flat in a suitcase and it is the souvenir people write to us about.

What can I do in Monteverde when it rains?

Let it rain, this is a cloud forest. A hot chocolate or a Cloud Forest Latte under the hanging plants is the local way to wait it out, and the forest is at its greenest right after. Our rainy day guide lists what stays open and what is better in the mist.

Do you have garden seating or a view?

Our tables sit under hanging plants, ferns and flowers, with the cloud forest air coming through. It is a garden more than a view: hummingbirds visit, and on clear afternoons the light comes in sideways through the mist.

Is Monteverde cold? What should I wear?

Cool, not cold: about 15 to 22 °C most of the year, with mist and wind. Bring layers and a light rain jacket even on sunny mornings. Then come in for something hot; we have coffee, tea and Costa Rican hot chocolate.

Do you accept credit cards, US dollars and colones?

Yes to all three. Prices are in colones; we also take dollars at the counter, cards, and SINPE Móvil. Change is given in colones.

Do you have wifi? Can I work on my laptop?

Yes, there is free wifi for guests; ask at the counter for the password. You are welcome to work a while. Mornings are the quietest and there are some outlets.

Do you have oat milk, almond milk or lactose free milk?

We have lactose free milk for any coffee or hot chocolate, just ask. We do not stock oat or almond milk. Every smoothie can be made in water instead of milk.

Do you have decaf?

No, we do not serve decaf. Our coffee is arabica from Monteverde, which is naturally lower in caffeine than most supermarket blends. For no caffeine at all, try the tea, the Costa Rican hot chocolate or a garden smoothie.

Do you have gluten free, vegan or vegetarian options?

Vegetarian, yes: the Monteverde sandwich, most pastries, drinks and smoothies. Vegan: smoothies in water, americano, tea and the sweet cane drink. Our kitchen is small, so we cannot promise zero contact with gluten; tell the team when you order and they will guide you.

Is the café dog friendly?

Well behaved dogs are welcome at the garden tables, and we will find them a bowl of water.

Is it good for kids?

Very. The garden gives children room to look at plants and hummingbirds, and the Costa Rican hot chocolate was practically made for them.

Is there parking?

Yes, free street parking right by the café. Mornings before 7:00 are the easiest time to find a spot.

Can I book a table, or bring a group?

No reservation needed, just come in. For groups of eight or more, or a small celebration, message us on WhatsApp a day ahead and we will set the garden for you.

Do you do takeaway, and can I order on WhatsApp?

Everything on the menu travels well; just ask for it to go. To order ahead, write to us on WhatsApp at +506 6400 6601 and tell us when you will pick it up.

Are you open on Sundays and holidays?

Yes. We open every day of the week, 6:00 to 19:00, holidays included. If a storm or a special day changes that, we post it on our Google profile first.

Is there a bathroom?

Yes, we have restrooms for guests.

Is tipping expected in Costa Rica?

Tips are never expected in Costa Rica and always appreciated. If someone made your morning, a small extra says so.

Do you speak English?

English y español, con mucho gusto. The menu, this website and our team speak both.
