# Basecamp Monteverde · research

Facts for the new website, each with its source and how sure we are.

## 0. How this was gathered, and its limits

Claude's cloud environment denies the host `basecampmonteverde.com`, so Claude has not loaded the live site.
The facts below come from:
- the search-engine index of the business's own pages, a snapshot from about mid-July 2026;
- the sister sites, orchidlodgemonteverde.com and hanginggardencafe.com;
- this repository's café documents, written with the owner;
- third-party travel sources.

Confidence labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | From the owner or this repository's owner-approved documents. |
| **Indexed** | From the business's own web pages, as stored by a search engine. May be out of date. |
| **Inferred** | Claude's conclusion from several clues. Needs the owner. |
| **Third-party** | From travel sites or guides. Check before quoting numbers. |

### Orchid Lodge policies (Indexed; candidates for Basecamp, BQ-14)

- Rates are per room, per night.
- 13% Costa Rican VAT applies to every booking.
- Children under 12 stay free when using existing bedding.
- Reservations are guaranteed with a valid credit card.
- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in.
- A non-refundable rate is 10% cheaper.
- Quiet hours 10 PM to 7 AM.
- No smoking indoors. No pets, except service animals.
- Group bookings accepted.
- Contact: stay@orchidlodgemonteverde.com, +506 2101-6980.

## 6. Monteverde facts for the Location and guide pages

All Third-party. Check numbers before publishing.

| Topic | What sources say |
|---|---|
| Elevation | About 1,300 to 1,440 m |
| Dry season | About mid-December to early May. Rain is possible all year. |
| Wettest months | September and October |
| Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve | Open 7:00 to 16:00 every day. Entry $25 in 2024. A bus runs from Santa Elena five times a day. |
| Rainy days | Hanging bridges and zip lines run in the rain unless there is lightning (café guide, Confirmed). |
| From San José airport (SJO) | Private transfer about 2 h 40 min. Shared shuttle about 3 to 4 h, roughly $55 to $69. |
| From Liberia airport (LIR) | Private about 3 h or more. Shared shuttle about 4 h, from about $70. |
| From La Fortuna | Jeep-boat-jeep across Lake Arenal, about 3 h, from about $43. |
