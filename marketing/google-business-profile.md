# Google Business Profile kit · Hanging Garden Café

Everything here is meant to be pasted into the Google Business Profile (business.google.com)
or printed. The website already answers the same questions at hanginggardencafe.com, so the
profile and the site confirm each other, which is what Google rewards.

## 1. Profile settings (check each one)

| Field | Set it to |
|---|---|
| Business name | Hanging Garden Café (exactly this, no "The", no slogans) |
| Primary category | Coffee shop |
| Additional categories | Café, Breakfast restaurant, Espresso bar |
| Phone | +506 6400 6601 |
| Website | https://hanginggardencafe.com/ |
| Menu link | https://hanginggardencafe.com/menu.html |
| Hours | Monday to Sunday 06:00 – 19:00 |
| Opening date | the real opening month and year |
| Description (750 characters max) | see below |
| Attributes | Serves coffee, Breakfast, Dine-in, Takeaway, Outdoor seating, Free wifi, Good for kids, Dogs allowed, Free street parking, Accepts credit cards, Accepts debit cards (add Wheelchair accessible and LGBTQ friendly if true) |
| Messaging | turn on WhatsApp / chat so people can write from the profile |

Description to paste:

> Coffee shop in Monteverde, Costa Rica, open every day from 6:00, one hour before the cloud forest reserve. We serve locally grown coffee from El Trapiche Monteverde, roasted nearby and ground for each cup: chorreado, espresso, our Cloud Forest Latte with honey and cinnamon. Breakfast sandwiches, empanadas, croissants, Costa Rican favourites like Tres Leches and Tamal Asado, and garden smoothies. Tables sit under hanging plants and ferns with hummingbirds passing through. Adventure Box to go for early tours. Cards, dollars and colones accepted. English y español.

## 2. Photos (first month: 30 to 50, then 3 to 5 every week)

Google ranks profiles with fresh photos higher and shows them in the map pack. In order of importance:

1. Logo (the round Hanging Garden Café emblem) and a cover photo of the front with the mural.
2. The bar window and the copper cup, the espresso machine, a chorreador being poured.
3. Six to eight drinks, each on its own: Cloud Forest Latte, Café con Leche, cappuccino, iced latte, hot chocolate, two smoothies.
4. Six food shots: Mano de Piedra, ham and cheese croissant, empanadas, Tres Leches, carrot cake, the Adventure Box packed.
5. The garden tables, the hanging plants, a hummingbird if you catch one, the view in the mist.
6. The team at the counter, smiling. People photos get the most clicks.
7. The interior at 6:00 with the lights on, so early risers know you are really open.

Take them on a phone in daylight, horizontal, no filters. Name the files before uploading, for example `hanging-garden-cafe-monteverde-cloud-forest-latte.jpg`.

## 3. Reviews: the plan

Target: 15 to 25 new reviews per month. Reply to every one within 48 hours, in the reviewer's language, mentioning something they said.

Get the review link: Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy the short link. Paste it into
`tools/pages/content.js` as `REVIEW_URL` and run `node tools/pages/build.js`; the website buttons then open the review box directly.

### Table card / receipt card (print, 9 × 5 cm, QR code to the review link)

Front:

> Did the coffee make your morning?
> Tell Google, it helps a small café in the clouds.
> [QR code]
> ¿Le gustó el café? Cuéntelo en Google, ayuda a un café pequeño entre las nubes.

Back:

> Hanging Garden Café · Monteverde · open every day 6:00 – 19:00 · WhatsApp +506 6400 6601 · hanginggardencafe.com

### WhatsApp follow-up (send the same afternoon to guests who ordered an Adventure Box or messaged you)

English:

> Hi! Thank you for visiting Hanging Garden Café today. If you have 30 seconds, a quick review on Google helps us more than you can imagine: [review link]. Pura vida, and safe travels.

Español:

> ¡Hola! Gracias por visitar Hanging Garden Café hoy. Si tiene 30 segundos, una reseña rápida en Google nos ayuda más de lo que imagina: [enlace de reseña]. Pura vida y buen viaje.

### Reply templates

Five stars: "Thank you, [name]. The [Cloud Forest Latte / sunrise coffee] is waiting for you next time you come up the mountain. Pura vida."

Four stars or less: "Thank you for telling us, [name]. We would like to make this right; please write to us on WhatsApp at +506 6400 6601 and the owner will reply personally."

Never argue in a reply, never offer a discount for a review, never buy reviews.

## 4. Questions and answers (paste into the profile's Q&A section, both languages)

Ask each question from your own account and answer it as the owner. These match the FAQ on the website word for word.

1. **What time do you open?** We open at 6:00 every day, one hour before the Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve. We close at 19:00. / **¿A qué hora abren?** Abrimos a las 6:00 todos los días, una hora antes de la reserva. Cerramos a las 19:00.
2. **Do you serve breakfast?** Yes, from 6:00: sandwiches, empanadas, croissants, banana bread and chorreado coffee. Adventure Box to go if you leave before we open. / **¿Sirven desayuno?** Sí, desde las 6:00: sándwiches, empanadas, croissants, pan de banano y café chorreado. Adventure Box para llevar si sale antes de que abramos.
3. **Where does your coffee come from?** El Trapiche, a family farm here in Monteverde, roasted locally and ground for each cup. / **¿De dónde es el café?** De El Trapiche, una finca familiar de Monteverde, tostado localmente y molido para cada taza.
4. **Do you accept credit cards and US dollars?** Yes: cards, dollars, colones and SINPE Móvil. / **¿Aceptan tarjetas y dólares?** Sí: tarjetas, dólares, colones y SINPE Móvil.
5. **Is there wifi?** Yes, free for guests; ask for the password at the counter. / **¿Hay wifi?** Sí, gratis; pida la clave en el mostrador.
6. **Is the café dog friendly?** Well behaved dogs are welcome at the garden tables. / **¿Aceptan perros?** Los perros educados son bienvenidos en las mesas del jardín.
7. **Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?** Vegetarian yes (the Monteverde sandwich, most pastries and smoothies); vegan: smoothies in water, americano, tea, sweet cane drink. / **¿Tienen opciones vegetarianas o veganas?** Vegetarianas sí; veganas: batidos en agua, americano, té y agua dulce.
8. **Can I book a table?** No reservation needed. Groups of eight or more: message us on WhatsApp a day ahead. / **¿Puedo reservar?** No hace falta. Grupos de ocho o más: escríbanos por WhatsApp un día antes.
9. **Do you do takeaway?** Everything travels well; order ahead on WhatsApp +506 6400 6601. / **¿Tienen para llevar?** Todo viaja bien; pida con anticipación por WhatsApp.
10. **How far are you from the cloud forest reserve?** About 10 minutes by car, on the road through Santa Elena. / **¿A qué distancia están de la reserva?** Unos 10 minutos en carro, sobre la carretera de Santa Elena.
11. **Can I buy coffee beans to take home?** Ask at the counter; we sell what we brew when we have it. / **¿Venden café en grano?** Pregunte en el mostrador; vendemos lo mismo que servimos cuando lo tenemos.
12. **Is there parking?** Yes, free street parking by the café. / **¿Hay parqueo?** Sí, gratis en la calle junto al café.
13. **Do you have lactose free milk or decaf?** Lactose free milk yes; no oat or almond milk; no decaf. / **¿Tienen leche deslactosada o descafeinado?** Deslactosada sí; sin leche de avena ni almendra; sin descafeinado.
14. **Are you open on Sundays and holidays?** Yes, every day 6:00 to 19:00. / **¿Abren domingos y feriados?** Sí, todos los días de 6:00 a 19:00.
