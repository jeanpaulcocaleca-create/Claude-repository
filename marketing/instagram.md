# Instagram kit · Hanging Garden Café

Two goals: be seen by the people who are already in Monteverde (or about to be), and turn that
attention into visits and WhatsApp orders. Everything here can be done from a phone.

## 1. Build the account right (30 minutes)

1. Sign up with the café email, not a personal one. Handle **@hanginggardencafe** so it matches
   the website. If it is taken: **@hanginggardencafe.cr**.
2. Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → **Business**, category
   **Coffee shop**. This unlocks contact buttons, insights and ads.
3. Profile
   - Photo: the round logo (`website/assets/logo-512.png` on the site).
   - Name field (searchable): `Hanging Garden Café · Monteverde`
   - Bio (150 characters max):
     > Coffee shop in the cloud forest ☕ Open daily 6:00–19:00, before the reserve · El Trapiche coffee · Santa Elena, Monteverde 🇨🇷 ☕ Café · Menú ⬇
   - Link: `https://hanginggardencafe.com`
   - Contact: WhatsApp +506 6400 6601, and the address exactly as on Google:
     50 metros sur y 50 metros este de la Iglesia, Provincia de Puntarenas, Santa Elena, 60109, Costa Rica
   - Action button: "Order food" → `https://hanginggardencafe.com/menu.html`
4. Create a **Facebook Page** with the same name and link both in Meta Business Suite (needed for ads
   and for the location). Add the café as a **location** so every post and every guest photo can be
   geotagged to it. Tourists standing in Santa Elena find places through geotags.
5. Story highlights, in this order: **Menu · Coffee · Find us · Adventure Box · Monteverde tips**.
   Covers in the cream and green of the logo.

## 2. Exposure without paying

**Day one:** post 9 pictures so the grid is not empty: the mural, the garden tables, a Café con Leche,
the Cloud Forest Latte, the Mano de Piedra, pastries, the chorreador pour, the mist over the road at
6:00, the team.

**Every week after that**
- 3 Reels, 7 to 15 seconds, vertical, filmed on the phone: the pour, the steam at dawn, rain on the
  leaves, a hummingbird. The coffee film from the website is the first Reel.
- 1 carousel taken from the guide pages on the website: "5 signs of a great cup",
  "What to do in Monteverde when it rains", "Breakfast before the reserve".
- Stories every day: today's special, the weather at 6:00 (visitors check this), a guest with
  permission, repost every guest story that tags the café.

**Every post**
- Geotag Hanging Garden Café.
- Caption in English first, then Spanish (templates below).
- 8 to 12 hashtags: one set below plus one about the dish.
- Tag and use **Collab** with El Trapiche, the hotels and the guides who send people.
- Table card: *"Tag @hanginggardencafe, we repost every morning"* does most of this work.

## 3. Advertising, small and precise

Start with 3 to 5 dollars a day in Meta Ads Manager, aimed only at people already in Monteverde.

| Campaign | Objective | Audience | Creative | Schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awareness, Reels placements | People *recently in* a 15 km radius around Santa Elena, ages 22 to 65, all languages | The coffee film cut to 12 s vertical, text on screen "Open at 6:00, before the reserve" | 5:30–9:00 and 15:00–19:00 |
| 2 | Engagement, click to WhatsApp | Same | Adventure Box photo, text "Order tonight, pick up from 6:30" | All day |
| Boost | Boost the best organic Reel each month | Same | That Reel | 10 dollars, 5 days |

Watch three numbers in Insights: **profile visits, "get directions" taps, WhatsApp messages**.
Stop anything that does not move them within two weeks.

## 4. When the account exists

Send the handle and it goes on the website footer and in the search data (schema `sameAs`), so
Google links the profile, the website and the Google Business Profile as the same café.
